Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Sewly ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our iOS application. Sewly is an AI-assisted sewing app that turns photos of garments into step-by-step sewing and repair plans.

Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. The App does not require an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, or contact details.

Photos You Submit

When you choose to generate a sewing or repair plan, the photo you select is sent securely to our server for the sole purpose of creating your plan. Photos are processed transiently, are not linked to your identity, and are not used for any other purpose. Your project photos and progress images are otherwise stored locally on your device only.

Body Measurements

If you choose to add body measurements, they are stored locally on your device and included only in your plan requests so results can be sized to you. They are never linked to your identity and you can remove them at any time in Settings.

Anonymous Usage Analytics

We collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (such as which screens are visited and which features are used) to improve the App. This data is tied to a random identifier, not to your identity, and contains no personal information, no photos, and no measurements.

Subscriptions and Payments

All payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, which processes an anonymous identifier and purchase state only, subject to RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use remote push notifications and no notification data leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: purchase processing
  • RevenueCat: subscription status management (anonymous identifier only)
  • Mixpanel: anonymous usage analytics

These services have their own privacy policies governing their data handling.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties beyond the services listed above, which are necessary for the App to function.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Data Security

All communication with our servers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your projects, chats, and measurements remain on your device.

Your Rights

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by deleting the App. Since we do not hold personal data about you, there is no server-side personal data to request or erase.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
